About Us

A salon built on
craft and trust.

Sochi Hair has been a cornerstone of the Melbourne hair scene since 1987. What began as a commitment to quality and genuine care has grown into a decades-long reputation for excellence.

We believe great hair starts with a great conversation. Our team takes the time to understand you — your lifestyle, your vision, and what makes you feel confident — before a single scissor is lifted.

Good to know

No-Show Policy

We are committed to providing superior quality services to our clients. Customers are asked to cancel any appointments that they will be unable to honour at least 24 hours in advance of their reservation. You may be required to telephone the salon directly to cancel.

Your Account

You will need to register an account to use our online booking service, with a valid email address and password. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account and for all activities that occur under it. Please provide only accurate, truthful information.

Eligibility

The online booking service is available to adults, or those above the age of consent. If you are under the age of consent, you must have the permission of an adult to make a booking.
